# YouTube Top Tips-and-Tricks Finder

Finds the most popular YouTube videos that teach tips and tricks about a
given topic (a game, tool, technique), ranks them by view count, and reports
the top few with title, channel, views, age, and URL. Stdlib only  ^`^t it scrapes
the public search-results page and parses the embedded JSON, so no `yt-dlp`,
API key, or browser is required. It does not download or transcribe video
content.

## When to Use

- "Find the most popular YouTube videos about X tips and tricks"
- "What are the top beginner tips videos for <game> on YouTube?"
- "Show me popular YouTube guides/tricks for <topic>, ranked by views"
- "I want to learn <topic>  ^`^t find the best tips videos to watch"
